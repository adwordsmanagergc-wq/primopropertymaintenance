Property Maintenance Services in Melbourne

From a single touch-up to a full exterior refresh, here's what we do best.

House Painting

Interior and exterior repaints with thorough preparation, premium paints and a clean, careful finish. Heritage and weatherboard homes a specialty.

High-Pressure Cleaning

Driveways, paths, patios, walls, fences and concrete surfaces cleaned thoroughly to lift dirt, oil, moss and discolouration.

House Wash Downs

Soft and pressure house washing to remove cobwebs, mould, dust and pollution from rendered, brick or weatherboard exteriors.

Deck Restoration

We sand, clean and re-coat timber decks with quality oils or stains to bring back depth, colour and weather protection.

Paver Cleaning & Sealing

Pressure clean, treat and seal pavers and pool surrounds to lock out stains, weeds and lichen for years.

Surface Sealing

Concrete, stone and tile sealing to protect against moisture damage, fading and stubborn stains.

Degreasing

Garage floors, driveways and commercial spaces degreased and deep-cleaned for a safer, sharper-looking surface.

Pool Cleaning

One-off and routine residential pool cleans, including tile lines, filters and water testing.
