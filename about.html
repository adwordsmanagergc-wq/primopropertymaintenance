About Primo Property Maintenance

Primo Property Maintenance is a local Glen Iris business specialising in painting, exterior cleaning and general property care across Melbourne's inner south-east.

We're proud to be small enough to care about every detail, and experienced enough to handle anything from a touch-up before auction to a full exterior repaint of a heritage home.

Our work is built on three simple things: turn up when we say we will, do the prep work properly, and leave the property cleaner than we found it.

  • Fully insured
  • ABN-registered Australian business
  • Free, no-obligation quotes
  • Quality materials & preparation
  • Friendly local team
